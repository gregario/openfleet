## Problem

### You're paying a toll on every vehicle you own.

Most fleet management software charges $20 to $60 per vehicle, per month. If you run 10 vans, that's $2,400 to $7,200 a year — for a map and some maintenance reminders. Add a vehicle, the bill goes up. Grow your business, get punished for it.

### You're locked into their hardware.

Samsara wants you on Samsara hardware. Verizon Connect wants you on Verizon hardware. Switch providers and your $2,000 worth of trackers becomes e-waste. Meanwhile, a $30 OBD-II dongle off Amazon does the same job.

### You're handing your business data to someone else's server.

Every route your drivers take, every job site they visit, every customer address — sitting on a vendor's cloud, governed by their terms of service, accessible until you stop paying. Miss a payment? Locked out of your own fleet history.

### The tools aren't even built for you.

Enterprise fleet platforms are designed for companies with 500 trucks and a dedicated fleet manager on salary. You don't need AI-powered route optimization or carbon offset dashboards. You need to know where Van 7 is, whether it's due for an oil change, and if the driver did the morning inspection. That's it.

---

## Solution

### Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

| Feature | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Live Map** | See every vehicle on a map, right now. Real-time position updates from any GPS source. |
| **Trip History** | Automatic trip detection and logging. Where did each vehicle go today, this week, this month. |
| **Maintenance Scheduling** | Set service intervals by mileage or date. Get notified before something breaks, not after. |
| **Driver Management** | Assign drivers to vehicles. Track license expiry, certifications, and contact details in one place. |
| **Inspections & Checklists** | Digital pre-trip and post-trip inspections. No more paper forms lost under the seat. |
| **Compliance Tracking** | Registration renewals, insurance expiry, inspection dates — all in one view, all with alerts. |
| **Any GPS Source** | Phone GPS, OBD-II dongle, dedicated tracker — if it sends coordinates, OpenFleet uses them. |
| **Built-in Simulator** | Try everything with a simulated fleet before connecting real vehicles. No hardware required. |
